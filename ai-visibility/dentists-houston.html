What AI tends to cite

For Houston dentists, AI answers usually pull from Google reviews and Google Business Profiles, Yelp and Healthgrades, Zocdoc, local “best dentist” lists, and Houston threads on Reddit. Mismatched details across listings or few recent reviews give the answers little to go on.

3 fixes that help

  1. Complete your Google Business Profile, including weekend hours and services, and ask patients for Google reviews.
  2. Keep your name, address, phone and hours identical on Zocdoc, Yelp and Healthgrades.
  3. Publish one page that answers what Houston patients ask: insurance accepted, new-patient availability, weekend hours, and prices.
