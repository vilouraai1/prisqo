What AI tends to cite for financial advisors

For financial advisors, AI answers usually pull from your own site, Google Business Profiles, professional directories and public registration records, local business press, and Reddit threads. Vague service pages, no explanation of how you are paid, or missing directory profiles leave the answers naming another firm.

3 fixes that help

  1. Complete your Google Business Profile and keep your firm name, address and phone the same everywhere, including professional directories.
  2. Explain on your site who you serve, what you do for them and how you are paid, in plain words.
  3. Publish one page per type of client or need, such as retirement planning or business owners, so answers have something specific to cite.
