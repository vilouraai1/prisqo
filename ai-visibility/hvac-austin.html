What AI tends to cite

For Austin HVAC companies, AI answers usually pull from Google reviews and Google Business Profiles, Yelp, Angi and HomeAdvisor, manufacturer dealer locators, Nextdoor, and Austin threads on Reddit. Few reviews or an unclear service area are often enough for the answers to name someone else.

3 fixes that help

  1. Claim your Google Business Profile, list every service and the Austin-area cities you cover, and ask customers for reviews.
  2. Keep your name, phone and service area identical on Angi, HomeAdvisor, Yelp and dealer listings.
  3. Publish one page per service, such as AC repair, new systems and maintenance plans, with response times and how you price.
