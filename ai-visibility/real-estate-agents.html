What AI tends to cite for real estate agents

For real estate agents, AI answers usually pull from Zillow and Realtor.com agent profiles, Google reviews, local news and market coverage, and community threads on Reddit. If your profiles have few reviews, no recent sales, or no local market content, the answers name an agent who does.

3 fixes that help

  1. Complete your Zillow and Realtor.com profiles and ask past clients for reviews on both, plus Google.
  2. Publish short local market guides on your site — one per neighborhood you serve — so the web has something of yours to cite.
  3. Keep your name, brokerage and phone identical everywhere, including your brokerage site and social profiles.
