What AI tends to cite for restaurants

For restaurants, AI answers usually pull from Google reviews and Google Business Profiles, Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, local food blogs and “best of” lists, and community threads on Reddit. If your menu is out of date, your photos are thin, or you are missing from the review sites, the answers name the place that is not.

3 fixes that help

  1. Keep your Google and Yelp profiles current — menu, hours, photos — and ask happy diners for reviews.
  2. Get listed on TripAdvisor and OpenTable, even if you take walk-ins only.
  3. Earn mentions in local food blogs and “best of” lists: invite a local writer in, or run one dish worth writing about.

Does ChatGPT recommend restaurants?

Yes. When asked for the best restaurant in a city, ChatGPT search looks up web results and names specific places with links. Which ones depends on reviews, listings and local coverage. Prisqo measures this with one AI model with live web search as a stand-in; it does not query ChatGPT directly.

Why isn't my restaurant named?

Usually your menu or hours are out of date, you have few recent reviews, or you are missing from the review sites and lists AI cites. A Prisqo check shows exactly which sources were cited for your category, so you can see where you are missing.
