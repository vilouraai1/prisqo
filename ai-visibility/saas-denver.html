What AI tends to cite

For software, AI answers usually pull from review sites like G2 and Capterra, Product Hunt launches, Reddit threads, and comparison articles on review blogs. Where you are based rarely matters to the answer; reviews and clear category pages do.

3 fixes that help

  1. Claim and complete your G2 and Capterra profiles with consistent naming and pricing, and ask happy customers for verified reviews.
  2. Publish one comparison page per rival you lose deals to, in the buyer’s words: features, price, and who each product suits.
  3. Answer the questions buyers type, such as pricing, integrations and free trial, on one short page each.

FAQ

Does ChatGPT recommend SaaS products from Denver companies?

It recommends products, not cities. Asked for the best software for a job, ChatGPT search looks up web results and names specific products with links, based on reviews, comparison pages and community mentions. Prisqo measures this with one AI model with live web search as a stand-in; it does not query ChatGPT directly.

Why isn’t my product named?

Usually you have few verified reviews on G2 or Capterra, your pages do not use the words buyers type, or competitors have more third-party mentions. A Prisqo check shows which sources were cited, so you can see where you are missing.
