What AI tends to cite

For Austin veterinarians, AI answers usually pull from Google reviews and Google Business Profiles, Yelp, pet directories, local “best of” lists, Nextdoor, and Austin threads on Reddit. Missing hours or few reviews give the answers little reason to name you.

3 fixes that help

  1. Complete your Google Business Profile with hours, emergency policy and services, and ask clients for reviews.
  2. Keep your clinic name, address, phone and hours identical on Yelp and pet directories.
  3. Publish pages for what Austin pet owners ask about, such as wellness plans, dental care or after-hours care, with how you price them.
