What AI tends to cite

For Austin wedding photographers, AI answers usually pull from The Knot and WeddingWire, Google reviews, venue preferred-vendor lists, Texas wedding blogs, and Austin threads on Reddit. Thin directory profiles or no venue pages leave the answers naming someone else.

3 fixes that help

  1. Complete your profiles on The Knot, WeddingWire and Google with your style and the Austin-area venues you shoot, and ask couples for reviews.
  2. Get listed on Hill Country and Austin venue preferred-vendor pages.
  3. Publish pages for the weddings you want, such as ranch venues, downtown weddings and Lake Travis elopements, with how booking works.
