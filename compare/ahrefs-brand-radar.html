Prisqo vs Ahrefs Brand Radar: which AI visibility tool fits a small business?

Last checked: 2026-11-10. Competitor pricing and features change often; check their sites before deciding.

Ahrefs Brand Radar tracks brand visibility across AI answers, built on an index of 551 million search-backed prompts. Prisqo is a much simpler product: a paid one-time check that answers “does AI recommend you?” for small businesses. Here’s an honest side-by-side so you can pick the right one.

The short answer

  • Choose Ahrefs Brand Radar if you want prompt tracking across AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot — with full responses, citations, share of voice and sentiment — and you are ready for a monthly subscription (from $50/mo, or bundled in a paid Ahrefs plan).
  • Choose Prisqo if you want a plain-English one-time answer to “does AI recommend my business, and who does it recommend instead?” at a small-business price, and a stand-in measurement is enough (one AI model with live web search, not ChatGPT itself).

Where Ahrefs Brand Radar is stronger

  • Direct engine coverage. It tracks six AI platforms directly and shows full responses with mentions and citations. Prisqo uses one search-grounded model as a stand-in.
  • Scale. A 551-million-prompt index of real search demand, plus sentiment and estimated impressions.
  • Bundled. Prompt tracking is free inside paid Ahrefs plans, so existing Ahrefs customers pay nothing extra for the entry tier.

Where Prisqo is different

  • Built for people who aren’t marketers. One score, the competitors beating you, the sources behind them, and what to do next, in plain English.
  • Re-check weekly, not daily. AI answers vary from run to run. Checking weekly focuses on real changes instead of daily noise.
  • A paid check, no sign-up. One report, pay once at checkout.
  • Local-business prompts. Prompts like "best [category] in [city]" are generated from your site.

What Prisqo measures

Prisqo doesn’t query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Grok or Copilot. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and city to one AI model with live web search, and records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. That’s a useful stand-in because the big assistants also look up web sources before recommending a business, and a search-grounded model shows which pages shaped its answer. It isn’t a copy of what ChatGPT says, though: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, so its answers can differ. See the FAQ for details.

Pricing in plain numbers

  • Ahrefs Brand Radar Prompt Tracking from $50/mo; AI Visibility Index from $199/mo.
  • Prisqo is one $19 report, not a subscription: the one-time check asks 6 prompts, answered by one AI model with live web search.

A note on accuracy (both tools)

Ahrefs Brand Radar queries the AI assistants it tracks. Prisqo asks one AI model with live web search instead, so its results are a stand-in for what ChatGPT or Perplexity would say, not a copy. Either way, automated results can differ from what you see when you type a question into an AI app yourself, because consumer apps personalize by location, memory and history. Prisqo shows the exact prompts and answers so you can check them.

FAQ

Is Prisqo an Ahrefs Brand Radar alternative? Yes, for small businesses that want a one-time report rather than a monthly dashboard. Teams that need direct tracking across six AI platforms, or an index of 551 million prompts, should look at Brand Radar.

Is Ahrefs Brand Radar free? There is a free preview, and prompt tracking is free inside paid Ahrefs plans. Standalone prompt tracking starts at $50/mo.

Which is cheaper? Brand Radar starts at $50/mo. Prisqo is $19 once for one report. Compare prompts, engines and frequency, not just price.
