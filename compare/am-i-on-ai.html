Prisqo vs Am I On AI: which AI visibility tool fits a small business?

Last checked: 2026-11-10. Competitor pricing and features change often; check their sites before deciding.

Am I On AI is an AI visibility monitor with weekly action plans: it shows whether your brand shows up when people ask ChatGPT and Google AI Overview for recommendations, with citation gaps, competitor benchmarks and personalized recommendations. Prisqo is a much simpler product: a paid one-time check that answers “does AI recommend you?” for small businesses. Here’s an honest side-by-side so you can pick the right one.

The short answer

  • Choose Am I On AI if you want ongoing tracking with a weekly action plan — and, as an agency, white-label tiers from $250/mo — and you are fine with ChatGPT and Google AI Overview as the only two engines.
  • Choose Prisqo if you want a plain-English one-time answer to “does AI recommend my business, and who does it recommend instead?” at a small-business price, and a stand-in measurement is enough (one AI model with live web search, not ChatGPT itself).

Where Am I On AI is stronger

  • Weekly action plans. Every scan comes with a personalized action plan, not just a score.
  • Agency tiers. White-label agency plans from $250/mo for 3 clients — the clearest agency path in this set.
  • Free trial. 7-day free trial, no commitment. Prisqo has no free tier.

Where Prisqo is different

  • Built for people who aren’t marketers. One score, the competitors beating you, the sources behind them, and what to do next, in plain English.
  • Re-check weekly, not daily. AI answers vary from run to run. Checking weekly focuses on real changes instead of daily noise.
  • A paid check, no sign-up. One report, pay once at checkout.
  • Local-business prompts. Prompts like "best [category] in [city]" are generated from your site.

What Prisqo measures

Prisqo doesn’t query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Grok or Copilot. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and city to one AI model with live web search, and records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. That’s a useful stand-in because the big assistants also look up web sources before recommending a business, and a search-grounded model shows which pages shaped its answer. It isn’t a copy of what ChatGPT says, though: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, so its answers can differ. See the FAQ for details.

Pricing in plain numbers

  • Am I On AI Pro is reported at $100/mo by third-party reads; the pricing page itself did not show a price in the copy we could read. Agency tiers are verified on the vendor’s agency page: Starter $250/mo for up to 3 clients.
  • Prisqo is one $19 report, not a subscription: the one-time check asks 6 prompts, answered by one AI model with live web search.

A note on accuracy (both tools)

Am I On AI queries the AI assistants it tracks. Prisqo asks one AI model with live web search instead, so its results are a stand-in for what ChatGPT or Perplexity would say, not a copy. Either way, automated results can differ from what you see when you type a question into an AI app yourself, because consumer apps personalize by location, memory and history. Prisqo shows the exact prompts and answers so you can check them.
