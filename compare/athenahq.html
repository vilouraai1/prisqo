Prisqo vs AthenaHQ: which AI visibility tool fits a small business?

Last checked: 2026-11-10. Competitor pricing and features change often; check their sites before deciding.

AthenaHQ is an AEO/GEO command center that pairs monitoring with action: it tracks visibility, sentiment and competitors, then runs optimization actions and a content agent. Prisqo is a much simpler product: a paid one-time check that answers “does AI recommend you?” for small businesses. Here’s an honest side-by-side so you can pick the right one.

The short answer

  • Choose AthenaHQ if you want monitoring plus the fix workflow — content optimization, citation intelligence and robots.txt/llms.txt management across 11 models — and you are ready for a credit-metered monthly subscription.
  • Choose Prisqo if you want a plain-English one-time answer to “does AI recommend my business, and who does it recommend instead?” at a small-business price, and a stand-in measurement is enough (one AI model with live web search, not ChatGPT itself).

Where AthenaHQ is stronger

  • Monitoring plus action. It does not just report visibility — it runs the optimization workflow, including a content agent and citation intelligence.
  • Engine breadth. 11 models on Starter. Prisqo uses one search-grounded model as a stand-in.
  • Free tier. An Essential free tier exists for trying it; Prisqo has no free tier.

Where Prisqo is different

  • Built for people who aren’t marketers. One score, the competitors beating you, the sources behind them, and what to do next, in plain English.
  • Re-check weekly, not daily. AI answers vary from run to run. Checking weekly focuses on real changes instead of daily noise.
  • A paid check, no sign-up. One report, pay once at checkout.
  • Local-business prompts. Prompts like "best [category] in [city]" are generated from your site.

What Prisqo measures

Prisqo doesn’t query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Grok or Copilot. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and city to one AI model with live web search, and records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. That’s a useful stand-in because the big assistants also look up web sources before recommending a business, and a search-grounded model shows which pages shaped its answer. It isn’t a copy of what ChatGPT says, though: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, so its answers can differ. See the FAQ for details.

Pricing in plain numbers

  • AthenaHQ Starter is reported at $295/mo by third-party reads; the pricing page itself did not show a price in the copy we could read. Treat $295 as reported, not verified, and check the page before deciding.
  • Prisqo is one $19 report, not a subscription: the one-time check asks 6 prompts, answered by one AI model with live web search.

A note on accuracy (both tools)

AthenaHQ queries the AI assistants it tracks. Prisqo asks one AI model with live web search instead, so its results are a stand-in for what ChatGPT or Perplexity would say, not a copy. Either way, automated results can differ from what you see when you type a question into an AI app yourself, because consumer apps personalize by location, memory and history. Prisqo shows the exact prompts and answers so you can check them.
