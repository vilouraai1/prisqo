Prisqo vs HubSpot AEO: which AI visibility tracker is right for you?

Last checked: 2026-11-03. Competitor pricing and features change often; check their sites before deciding.

HubSpot now has two AI-visibility products: the free AI Search Grader (one-time check) and HubSpot AEO (ongoing tracking). Prisqo does the same job more simply: a paid one-time check, with a focus on small and local businesses and the agencies that serve them. Here's the honest comparison.

The short answer

  • Choose HubSpot AEO if you want tracking of ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity themselves, you already use HubSpot, or you want a daily-updated score with share of voice, sentiment and citation analysis, and like having it in the same ecosystem as your CRM. (It doesn't require HubSpot's CMS, per HubSpot's FAQ.)
  • Use HubSpot's free AI Search Grader for a one-off brand-perception snapshot.
  • Choose Prisqo if you want to see which competitors AI recommends instead of you for local "best [service] in [city]" questions, without joining a larger platform, and a stand-in measurement is enough (one AI model with live web search, not ChatGPT itself).

Where HubSpot is stronger

  • Direct engine coverage. HubSpot AEO tracks ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity themselves. Prisqo doesn't query ChatGPT or Perplexity; it uses one AI model with live web search as a stand-in. If you need to know what ChatGPT itself says, HubSpot is the better choice.
  • Brand and ecosystem. If your team lives in HubSpot, AEO sits next to your CRM, and HubSpot says Marketing Hub Pro/Enterprise includes expanded AEO prompts and CRM-powered recommendations.
  • Daily score updates and sentiment analysis.
  • Free one-time grader with a 5-dimension breakdown.
  • Investment. HubSpot says it acquired XFunnel in 2026 to build its AEO tools (hubspot.com/products/aeo).

Where Prisqo is different

  • "Who's beating me?" is the headline, not a sub-metric: every report leads with the businesses AI recommends instead of you, and the sources it cites for them.
  • Local-business prompts out of the box, e.g. "best family dentist in Austin".
  • Built for agencies with many small clients: one plan, several brands, vs one HubSpot AEO instance per brand.

What Prisqo measures

Prisqo doesn't query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Grok or Copilot. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and city to one AI model with live web search, and records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. That's a useful stand-in because the big assistants also look up web sources before recommending a business, and a search-grounded model shows which pages shaped its answer. It isn't a copy of what ChatGPT says, though: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, so its answers can differ. See the FAQ for details.

FAQ

Is HubSpot's AI Search Grader free? Yes. It's a free, one-time check (hubspot.com/ai-search-grader, accessed 2026-11-03).

How much is HubSpot AEO? $50/month, or $45/month paid annually, with 25 prompts, and you can buy more. There's a free trial with no credit card (hubspot.com/products/aeo, accessed 2026-11-03).

Do I need HubSpot's CMS to use HubSpot AEO? HubSpot says no.

What's the difference between Prisqo and HubSpot's grader? The grader gives a general brand score. Prisqo is a one-time $19 report: it asks 6 buyer questions for your category and city, and shows whether AI names you, who it names instead, and the sites to get listed on.
