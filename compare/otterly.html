Prisqo vs Otterly.AI: which AI visibility tool fits a small business?

Last checked: 2026-11-03. Competitor pricing and features change often; check their sites before deciding.

Otterly.AI is a well-established AI search monitoring platform used by marketing teams and agencies. Prisqo is a much simpler product: a paid one-time check that answers "does AI recommend you?" for small businesses and the agencies that serve them. Here's an honest side-by-side so you can pick the right one.

The short answer

  • Choose Otterly.AI if you need to know what the AI assistants themselves say. It tracks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Copilot directly, with Gemini, AI Mode and Claude as add-ons. Also choose it for daily tracking, content audits, prompt research, an API, and you have a marketing team to act on the data.
  • Choose Prisqo if you want a plain-English one-time answer to "does AI recommend my business, and who does it recommend instead?" at a small-business price, and a stand-in measurement is enough (one AI model with live web search, not ChatGPT itself).

Where Otterly.AI is stronger

  • Direct engine coverage. Otterly tracks the assistants themselves: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot, with Gemini, AI Mode and Claude as add-ons. Prisqo doesn't query any of those apps. It uses one AI model with live web search as a stand-in. If you need to know exactly what ChatGPT or Perplexity tells people, Otterly is the better choice.
  • Daily data. It's better for brands running frequent content or PR campaigns that need fast feedback.
  • Depth. Prompt research, content audits, AI crawler simulation and an API.
  • Track record. Otterly cites a 2025 Gartner Cool Vendor recognition and says it's used by 40,000+ marketing professionals (Otterly's own claims, otterly.ai/, accessed 2026-11-03).

Where Prisqo is different

  • Built for people who aren't marketers. One score, the competitors beating you, the sources behind them, and what to do next, in plain English.
  • Re-check weekly, not daily. AI answers vary from run to run. Checking weekly focuses on real changes instead of daily noise.
  • A paid check, no sign-up. One report, pay once at checkout.
  • Local-business prompts. Prompts like "best [category] in [city]" are generated from your site.

What Prisqo measures

Prisqo doesn't query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Grok or Copilot. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and city to one AI model with live web search, and records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. That's a useful stand-in because the big assistants also look up web sources before recommending a business, and a search-grounded model shows which pages shaped its answer. It isn't a copy of what ChatGPT says, though: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, so its answers can differ. See the FAQ for details.

Pricing in plain numbers

  • Otterly Lite: $29/mo for 15 prompts on 4 engines; adding Gemini/AI Mode is +$9/mo on Lite (Otterly pricing page).
  • Prisqo is one $19 report, not a subscription: the one-time check asks 6 prompts, answered by one AI model with live web search.

A note on accuracy (both tools)

Otterly queries the AI assistants it tracks. Prisqo asks one AI model with live web search instead, so its results are a stand-in for what ChatGPT or Perplexity would say, not a copy. Either way, automated results can differ from what you see when you type a question into an AI app yourself, because consumer apps personalize by location, memory and history. Otterly says this too in its FAQ (https://otterly.ai/). Prisqo shows the exact prompts and answers so you can check them.

FAQ

Is Prisqo an Otterly.AI alternative? Yes, for small businesses and agencies that want a one-time report rather than a daily GEO platform. Teams that need direct tracking of ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews or Copilot, or an API, should look at Otterly.

Is Otterly.AI free? Otterly offers a free trial with no credit card required. Paid plans start at $29/month (Otterly pricing page, accessed 2026-11-03).

Which is cheaper? Otterly Lite is $29/mo. Prisqo is $19 once for one report. Compare prompts, engines and frequency, not just price.
