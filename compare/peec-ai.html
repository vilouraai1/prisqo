Prisqo vs Peec AI: a simpler, small-business alternative

Last checked: 2026-11-03. Competitor pricing and features change often; check their sites before deciding.

Peec AI is a full AI search analytics platform for SEO and marketing teams, with daily tracking, source analytics and an agency program. Prisqo answers one question for small businesses, "does AI recommend you, and who does it recommend instead?", in a one-time report. Here's how they compare.

The short answer

  • Choose Peec AI if you're an SEO/marketing team or agency that needs daily data across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode/Overviews, Copilot and Gemini, plus source gap analysis, multiple countries, an API/MCP, Looker Studio and SOC 2.
  • Choose Prisqo if you're a small business (or a small agency) that wants a paid check and a plain-English one-time report with the competitors and sources behind your AI visibility, and a stand-in measurement is enough (one AI model with live web search, not ChatGPT itself).

Where Peec AI is stronger

  • Depth of analytics: source gap analysis, fan-out queries, brand perception and fact-checking, AI shopping/SKU tracking, crawl insights.
  • Direct engine tracking: Peec queries the assistants themselves, including ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, Copilot and Gemini (Perplexity, Claude and Grok on Enterprise). Prisqo doesn't query any consumer AI app. It uses one AI model with live web search as a stand-in. If you need exact ChatGPT or AI Overviews results, Peec is the better choice.
  • Scale: multiple projects and countries, API, SSO on Enterprise, SOC 2.
  • Agency tooling: pitch projects and client dashboards. Peec says it's trusted by 4,000+ brands and agencies and rated 4.7/5 on G2 (Peec's own claims, accessed 2026-11-03).

Where Prisqo is different

  • Price point for one location or one brand. A business that wants a one-time read on whether AI answers recommend it may not need 50 daily prompts across 3 models.
  • Plain-English report instead of a dashboard.
  • Paid check with no account.

What Prisqo measures

Prisqo doesn't query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Grok or Copilot. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and city to one AI model with live web search, and records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. That's a useful stand-in because the big assistants also look up web sources before recommending a business, and a search-grounded model shows which pages shaped its answer. It isn't a copy of what ChatGPT says, though: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, so its answers can differ. See the FAQ for details.

FAQ

How much does Peec AI cost? Starter is $95/mo, Pro $245/mo, Advanced $495/mo, Enterprise custom (peec.ai/pricing, accessed 2026-11-03). Older Reddit threads quote lower euro prices, which appear out of date.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Peec AI? Prisqo is $19 once for one report. Otterly.AI Lite is $29/mo and HubSpot AEO is $50/mo (see our other comparisons). Each trades depth for price.

Does Peec AI track Perplexity? On the brand pricing page accessed 2026-11-03, Perplexity is listed under Enterprise only.
