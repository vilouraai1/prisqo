Prisqo vs Profound: which AI visibility tool fits a small business?

Last checked: 2026-11-10. Competitor pricing and features change often; check their sites before deciding.

Profound is an enterprise AI visibility platform built around an AI Marketer teammate: it researches, writes, reports and collaborates on AI search. Prisqo is a much simpler product: a paid one-time check that answers “does AI recommend you?” for small businesses. Here’s an honest side-by-side so you can pick the right one.

The short answer

  • Choose Profound if you are an enterprise team that wants an AI Marketer agent, Prompt Volumes demand data, AI-traffic attribution, and tracking across up to 9 answer engines — and you are ready for a sales-led, custom-priced plan.
  • Choose Prisqo if you want a plain-English one-time answer to “does AI recommend my business, and who does it recommend instead?” at a small-business price, and a stand-in measurement is enough (one AI model with live web search, not ChatGPT itself).

Where Profound is stronger

  • Enterprise depth. AI Marketer agents, Prompt Volumes demand data, and AI-sourced traffic attribution go beyond monitoring.
  • Engine breadth. Up to 9 answer engines on Enterprise, against Prisqo’s one search-grounded model.
  • Built for teams. Team credits, SSO/SAML and dedicated support on Enterprise.

Where Prisqo is different

  • Built for people who aren’t marketers. One score, the competitors beating you, the sources behind them, and what to do next, in plain English.
  • Re-check weekly, not daily. AI answers vary from run to run. Checking weekly focuses on real changes instead of daily noise.
  • A paid check, no sign-up. One report, pay once at checkout.
  • Local-business prompts. Prompts like "best [category] in [city]" are generated from your site.

What Prisqo measures

Prisqo doesn’t query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Grok or Copilot. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and city to one AI model with live web search, and records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. That’s a useful stand-in because the big assistants also look up web sources before recommending a business, and a search-grounded model shows which pages shaped its answer. It isn’t a copy of what ChatGPT says, though: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, so its answers can differ. See the FAQ for details.

Pricing in plain numbers

  • Profound has no published self-serve price: a 7-day free trial (50 prompts a day), then Enterprise by custom quote.
  • Prisqo is one $19 report, not a subscription: the one-time check asks 6 prompts, answered by one AI model with live web search.

A note on accuracy (both tools)

Profound queries the AI assistants it tracks. Prisqo asks one AI model with live web search instead, so its results are a stand-in for what ChatGPT or Perplexity would say, not a copy. Either way, automated results can differ from what you see when you type a question into an AI app yourself, because consumer apps personalize by location, memory and history. Prisqo shows the exact prompts and answers so you can check them.

FAQ

Is Prisqo a Profound alternative? Yes, for small businesses that want a one-time report rather than an enterprise platform. Teams that need an AI Marketer agent, prompt-volume data or AI-traffic attribution should talk to Profound.

Is Profound free? Profound offers a 7-day free trial (50 prompts a day). There is no free tier and no published paid price; Enterprise is custom-quoted.

Which is cheaper? Profound has no published price, so compare the trial against Prisqo’s $19 once for one report — and ask Profound for a quote before deciding.
