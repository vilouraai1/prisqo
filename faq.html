AI visibility FAQ: how AI assistants choose who to recommend

About AI recommendations

Does ChatGPT recommend local businesses?

Yes. When someone asks ChatGPT something like "best plumber in Denver", it often searches the web and names specific businesses with links to its sources. OpenAI says ChatGPT search rewrites your question into search queries and may use third-party search providers (OpenAI Help Center). Whether your business is named depends on what those sources say about you.

Why is my business not showing up in ChatGPT?

Usually it's one of these: your site blocks or isn't reachable by AI search crawlers (ChatGPT search relies on OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot being allowed), you're missing from the directories, review sites and "best of" articles that AI cites for your category, your business name, address and description differ across the web, or competitors simply have more third-party mentions. A Prisqo check shows which sources a search-grounded AI model cites for your category, so you can see where you may be missing.

How do I get ChatGPT to recommend my business?

There's no paid placement or guaranteed method. What tends to help: allow AI search crawlers in robots.txt, keep your name, address, phone and description identical everywhere, get listed and reviewed on the sites AI cites for your category, earn mentions in relevant "best [category] in [city]" articles and community discussions, and publish clear pages that answer customer questions. Then re-check weekly to see what moved.

Is AI visibility the same as SEO?

They overlap. AI search tools rely on web search results and crawled pages, so good SEO helps. But AI answers name only a few businesses, they lean heavily on third-party sources like reviews, directories, Reddit and articles, and they can change from one run to the next. That's why AI visibility is tracked separately.

Which AI assistants matter for a small business?

The best-known today are ChatGPT, Google's AI answers (AI Overviews and AI Mode), Perplexity, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot. Prisqo doesn't query ChatGPT, Perplexity or Copilot directly, and doesn't track Google AI Overviews or AI Mode. It measures using one AI model with live web search as a stand-in. See the next question for what that means.

Does Prisqo check ChatGPT?

No. Prisqo doesn't query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or Grok, and it can't promise what ChatGPT will say about you. It asks buyer-style questions about your category and location to one AI model with live web search, then records who gets recommended and which sources are cited. Why that's a useful stand-in: ChatGPT search and Perplexity also look things up on the web before naming businesses, and a model with live web search returns the sources behind its answer, so you can see which pages shape AI recommendations in your category. The fixes that tend to help are the same everywhere: be reachable by crawlers, consistent across the web, and mentioned on the sites AI cites. Why it isn't identical: ChatGPT uses its own index and search partners, and every AI's answers vary from run to run, so treat Prisqo's results as a directional signal, not a ChatGPT ranking.

Prisqo's score runs from 0 to 100 and reflects how often, and how prominently, the AI answers we collect recommend your business across the questions checked. What Prisqo measures (and what it doesn't)

We don't check ChatGPT directly. Prisqo doesn't query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or Grok, and it can't see what any of them told a particular customer. We ask buyer-style questions about your category and location to one AI model with live web search, then record who is recommended and which sources are cited.

Why that's a useful stand-in:

  • The big assistants look things up before they answer. OpenAI says ChatGPT search turns your question into web searches and can use third-party search providers. Perplexity runs its own web search. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode are built on Google Search. So for "best [service] in [city]" questions, what the web says about you (reviews, directories, local "best of" articles, community threads) shapes the answer.
  • An AI model with live web search works the same basic way, and shows its sources. It looks up current web results before it answers and returns the pages it used. That means we can show you which pages shaped the answer, which is the part you can actually act on.

The limits, plainly:

  • It's a stand-in, not a copy. Independent analysis reported by Search Engine Land found ChatGPT relies heavily on OpenAI's own index and doesn't simply mirror any one search engine, so ChatGPT can name different businesses than we do.
  • AI answers vary from one run to the next, and consumer apps personalize by location and history.
  • We show the exact questions and answers so you can check them, and we track trends week to week rather than trusting any single answer.
