How agencies can check a client’s AI visibility

Clients are starting to ask whether ChatGPT and other AI assistants recommend them. Here is a simple way to check for a client business and present what you find, without new software or a subscription.

Get my report ($19)

14-day refund · One-time charge · Report ready in minutes

Start with the questions buyers ask

Clients rarely search for themselves by name, and neither do their customers. Think in the shape of a buyer’s question: a category and a place, sometimes with a need attached. Those are the questions to check.

Run one report per client business

Enter the client’s business name, website, category and city at the check form, pay $19 once, and Prisqo asks 6 buyer-style questions to one AI model with live web search. The report shows whether the client was named, who was named instead and which sites the answers cited. Each report covers one business.

Read the cited sites first

The cited sites are where the answer looked, and they are the part you can act on. If competitors appear on review sites, directories or local lists where your client is missing, that is the work plan.

Present it in the report’s own format

The report lives at its own link, so you can send it to the client as it is or walk them through it on a call. Look at the sample report to see the layout: a visibility score, who AI recommends instead, the sources cited and every question asked.

Turn it into next steps

Pair each gap with a fix: claim and complete listings, align name, address and phone, get reviews on the sites that were cited, and give each service a clear page. The checklist guide lists these in order.

What to fix first

Run a report for one client you know well, read the cited sites, and compare them with where the client is listed. That gives you a concrete conversation for the next meeting. See the for agencies page for how reports work for client accounts.
