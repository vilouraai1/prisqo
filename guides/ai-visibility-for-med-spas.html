AI visibility for med spas

Clients now ask AI assistants things like “best place for lip filler in Scottsdale” or “who does laser hair removal near me”. The assistant reads the web and names a few med spas. Here is what those answers tend to cite, and how to check whether yours is named.

How clients ask

Clients usually ask by treatment name and city, sometimes with a detail such as who performs it or how long results last. Each question favors med spas with a clear page for that treatment.

Treatment pages

One page per treatment, naming your city and explaining who it suits, what to expect and who performs it, gives answers something precise to quote.

Provider bios

Bios with each provider’s credentials and the treatments they perform help answers name your med spa when clients ask for an experienced injector or a licensed provider.

Reviews

Answers often cite Google reviews, RealSelf, Yelp and local “best of” lists. Reviews that mention the treatment by name match the way clients ask.

What to fix first

Ask a few client-style questions for your main treatments and city with search on and note which sites are cited. Then read the med spas page for the fixes to start with.
