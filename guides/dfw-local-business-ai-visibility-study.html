DFW local business AI visibility study

In November 2026 we asked one search-grounded AI model for the best provider in 71 city-and-category combinations across Dallas–Fort Worth and nearby cities. This page reports exactly what came back — no estimates, no quality ranking.

What we asked

71 checks, each one a single question of the form “best <category> in <city>, TX”, asked once to one search-grounded AI model. 69 checks ran on Nov 13, 2026 (Gemini) and 2 ran on Nov 11, 2026 (both in Lewisville). The model answered from live web search and named the businesses it recommended, with links. Prisqo does not query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or Grok directly.

37 of the 71 checks cover six Dallas–Fort Worth cities: Dallas, Plano, Frisco, Lewisville, Irving and Arlington. The other 34 cover nearby cities (Allen, Carrollton, Denton, Flower Mound, Fort Worth, Grapevine, Highland Village, Keller, McKinney, Southlake, The Colony). 18 categories were checked, from dentists and plumbers to med spas and auto repair.

The shortlist is tiny: a median of 5 names per question

Across all 71 checks, the AI named 349 businesses in total — a median of 5 per question, ranging from 2 to 10. The DFW numbers are the same: 37 checks, 185 mentions, median 5.

Almost nobody repeats

Across the 37 DFW checks, 185 mentions covered 182 different businesses. Only three businesses were named in more than one DFW check:

  • Christian Brothers Automotive — auto repair, Arlington and Irving (Nov 13, 2026).
  • Mullen & Mullen Law Firm — personal injury, Dallas and Plano (Nov 13, 2026).
  • Berkeys Air Conditioning, Plumbing & Electrical — plumbing in Dallas and HVAC in Irving (Nov 13, 2026).

Across all 71 checks the picture is the same: 349 mentions, 337 different businesses. There is no small club of usual winners — the named set is local and fragmented, different in every city.

The thinnest answers

Four checks named 3 or fewer businesses. The smallest answers in the snapshot:

  • Lewisville family law — 2 named (Nov 11, 2026): Jennifer Ilarraza (Ilarraza Law), William F. Neal.
  • Lewisville personal injury — 3 named (Nov 11, 2026): Kuzmich Law Firm, Shuster Law, Just Wrecked Law.
  • Highland Village physical therapy — 3 named (Nov 13, 2026).
  • McKinney HVAC — 3 named (Nov 13, 2026).

The largest answers were Irving auto repair (10 named, Nov 13, 2026) and Carrollton auto detailing (7 named, Nov 13, 2026).

What the named businesses have in common

Honestly: this snapshot cannot say. It records names only — no review counts, no profile completeness, no ranking signals — so any claim about what the winners share would be invented. The only pattern the data actually shows is fragmentation: 182 different businesses across 185 DFW mentions. If there is a shared trait, it is not visible in a name list; it lives in the sources the model read that day (reviews, directories, local lists), which is exactly what a Prisqo report records for your own business.

FAQ

How was this study done?

We ran 71 checks in November 2026: each check asked one search-grounded AI model for the best provider in one category and city (“best dentist in Dallas, TX”). 69 checks ran Nov 13, 2026 and 2 ran Nov 11, 2026, both in Lewisville. Each question was asked once, and answers change from run to run, so treat these as snapshots from those dates, not permanent rankings.

Does being unnamed mean my business is bad?

No. The snapshot records what one AI model returned on one day for one question. A business can be excellent and still unnamed if the model’s sources that day didn’t surface it. It does mean the business was invisible in that answer — and that answer is what the asker saw.

Which businesses were named most often?

Almost none repeated. Across the 37 DFW checks, 185 mentions covered 182 different businesses. Only three were named twice: Christian Brothers Automotive (auto repair, Arlington and Irving), Mullen & Mullen Law Firm (personal injury, Dallas and Plano), and Berkeys Air Conditioning, Plumbing & Electrical (plumbing in Dallas, HVAC in Irving).
