Does ChatGPT recommend my business? How to check

You can find out for free in about fifteen minutes. Here is how to check by hand in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, how to read what comes back, and when it is worth paying $19 to have it done for you.

Ask the questions your customers ask

Customers rarely type your business name. They ask for a category and a place, sometimes with a need attached. Open ChatGPT (with search on), Perplexity and Gemini, and try questions like these, swapping in your trade and city:

  1. best dentist in Austin, TX
  2. top rated plumber near Lake Highlands, Dallas
  3. who is the most trusted real estate agent in Chicago?
  4. affordable med spa in Houston for Botox
  5. best CRM for a small accounting firm

Use a private or logged-out window where you can, so past chats do not color the answer. Ask each question as a fresh conversation, and write down what each assistant says.

What to record from each answer

For every answer, note four things: whether your business is named at all, where it appears in the list, which competitors are named instead, and which sites the answer cites or links to. The cited sites matter most. They tell you where the assistant looked, and those are the places you can actually influence: review sites, directories, local lists, forums and news.

What the answers mean

If you are named near the top in most answers, the web already says clear, consistent things about you. If you are named sometimes, or low in the list, you are in the running but competitors have more or fresher third-party proof. If you are never named, the assistant probably has little to go on: you may be missing from the sources it cites for your trade, or your details differ across listings. The dentists, plumbers and lawyers pages list the sources AI tends to cite for each trade.

Why results vary from run to run

The same question can get a different answer an hour later. Assistants search the web live, the pages they find change, and the model words its answer a little differently each time. Location, phrasing and whether search is switched on all shift the result. So treat one answer as a snapshot. A fair picture comes from several questions, asked fresh, and read together.

FAQ

Does ChatGPT recommend local businesses?

Yes. With search on, ChatGPT looks up web results and names specific businesses, often with links. Which ones depends on what review sites, directories and local pages say about them.

Why does ChatGPT name my competitor and not me?

Usually the competitor appears on more of the sites the answer cites, has more recent reviews, or has consistent details everywhere. Compare the cited sites with where you are listed.
