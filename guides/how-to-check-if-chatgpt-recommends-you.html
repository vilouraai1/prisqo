How to check if ChatGPT recommends your business

You can test whether AI assistants name your business in a few minutes. Here is a simple way to do it yourself, why one test can mislead you, and what a Prisqo report does for you instead.

Step one: write the questions your buyers ask

Write a few questions the way a customer would, not the way you describe yourself: “best family dentist in Plano”, “plumber open on weekends near me”. Include your city or area and the service, and leave your business name out.

Step two: ask with search on

Ask each question in ChatGPT with search on, then in Perplexity and Gemini. Use a private or logged-out window where you can, so past chats don’t steer the answer.

Step three: note who is named and what is cited

For each answer, write down whether you were named, who was named instead, and which sites the answer cites. The cited sites tell you where to show up.

Why one test is noisy

Answers are written fresh each time, so wording, location, timing and your own history can change them. One answer is a snapshot. Look at several questions together before drawing conclusions.

Can I check myself whether ChatGPT recommends my business?

Yes. Ask a few buyer-style questions with search on, in a private window, and note who is named and which sites are cited. Repeat across questions, since one answer can vary.

Why did I get a different answer than a friend?

Answers are written fresh each time and can shift with wording, location, timing and each person’s history. That is why several questions tell you more than one.
