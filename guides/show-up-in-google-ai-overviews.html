How to show up in Google AI Overviews

AI Overviews are the AI-written summaries Google shows above some search results, with links to the pages they draw on. Here is how they tend to pick sources, and what a small business can do to be one of them.

How AI Overviews pick sources

AI Overviews are built from pages Google already indexes and ranks. They tend to cite pages that answer the question directly and clearly, from sites that look trustworthy on the topic. For local questions, Google’s own business listings and reviews play a large part, alongside directories and review sites.

Answer the questions people actually search

Write pages that answer the questions customers ask, in plain words, near the top of the page: what you do, where, what it costs or how pricing works, how long it takes. A short FAQ on a service page gives the summary something specific to quote.

Make your Google Business Profile complete

For local searches, your Google Business Profile is one of the main things Google can draw on. Fill in every field, choose the right categories, list services, keep hours current, add photos and ask customers for reviews.

Be findable and readable

A page can only be cited if Google can crawl and index it. Make sure important pages aren’t blocked, load quickly, work on phones and have clear titles and headings. Structured data such as LocalBusiness and FAQ markup helps Google understand what a page is about.

Earn mentions elsewhere

Mentions on local news sites, trade directories, review sites and community pages make your business look established on the topic. These are the same signals that help in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers.

What to fix first

Pick your three most important services and make sure each has its own page that answers the obvious questions in its first paragraph. Then complete your Google Business Profile. The AI search visibility checklist covers the rest in order.

FAQ

Can I pay to appear in Google AI Overviews?

No. Ads may appear near them, but the sources an AI Overview cites are not paid placements.

Does showing up in AI Overviews help with ChatGPT too?

Often. Clear pages, a complete business profile, consistent listings and mentions on trusted sites help across AI answers, since they all read the web.
