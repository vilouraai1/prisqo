Why doesn’t ChatGPT mention my business?

If you ask ChatGPT for the best business like yours in your city and you aren’t named, there is usually a short list of reasons. Here they are, roughly in the order they tend to matter, with what to fix first.

Your site doesn’t say plainly what you do and where

AI assistants read your pages the way a stranger would. If your homepage is mostly slogans, or your city and services are buried, the answer has little to quote. Put one clear sentence near the top: what you do, for whom, and where. Give each main service its own page.

Few other sites mention you

When an assistant searches the web, it leans on what other sites say: review sites, directories, local news, “best of” lists and forums. If you only appear on your own site, you look unproven next to a competitor that shows up in several places.

Your name, address or phone differ across listings

If your business name is spelled three ways, or an old address still sits on one directory, the answer has to guess which one is right. Assistants tend to name businesses whose details agree everywhere.

No reviews or listings an AI can cite

Reviews and listings are the evidence an answer points to. A missing or half-empty Google Business Profile, or no presence on the review sites people in your trade use, gives the answer nothing to link.

Crawlers are blocked

Some sites block AI crawlers in robots.txt or behind a security setting without meaning to. If assistants can’t read your pages, they can only rely on what other sites say about you. Check your robots.txt and any bot-blocking settings with whoever runs your site.

What to fix first

Start with what you control fastest: make your homepage say what you do and where, claim and complete your Google Business Profile, and fix any listing that shows an old name, address or phone. Then work on reviews and mentions on the sites AI cites for your trade.

FAQ

Why does ChatGPT recommend my competitor and not me?

Usually the competitor appears on more of the sites the answer cites, has more recent reviews, or has the same details everywhere. Compare the sites that get cited with where you are listed.

Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT?

No. There is no paid placement in ChatGPT’s answers. What helps is clear site copy, consistent listings, reviews and mentions on the sites it cites.
