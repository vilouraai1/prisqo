Your competitors are being recommended instead

When a customer asks an AI assistant for the best dentist, plumber, salon or lawyer in their city, it names a few businesses — and the customer usually calls the first one it names. If it does not name you, that customer never reaches you.

  • AI answers name only a few businesses per question, and they lean on the review sites, directories and “best of” pages they already trust.
  • If your business is missing from those pages, or your name and details differ across the web, AI has little to say about you — and says plenty about your competitors.
  • In our sample report for a fictional dentist, AI named Sunrise Dentist in 5 of 6 answers. That is example data, not a real business — but it shows the shape of the problem: the named business gets the call.
