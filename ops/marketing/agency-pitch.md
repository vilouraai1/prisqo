## The pitch (for the page or a call)

Your clients' customers are asking AI assistants who to call — and AI answers with names, not a list of links. A five-star business can still be invisible in the answer.

Prisqo is a one-time $19 report that shows a local business whether AI recommends it, who it names instead, and what to fix. Six real buyer questions, one AI model with live web search, no account, no subscription. The report lives at its own web address — nothing is emailed.

For a small SEO or marketing agency, it is a foot-in-the-door audit: run one for a prospect, walk in with the answer, and the upsell writes itself. Buy the report at $19 and resell or bundle it at your price. No minimums, no contract, no dashboard to learn.

What it is not: not a subscription, not rank tracking, not a promise to make AI recommend anyone. It is a snapshot of what AI says today, and the fix list that follows from it.
