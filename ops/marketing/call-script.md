# Call script: Jilsha, VilouraAI

Use with `ops/marketing/call-list.csv`. Work top-down: phone-only leads come first. Before each call, glance at the row's `hook` and use it in the "why I'm calling" line. If the hook is blank, skip that line.

Free offers: the "Does AI recommend your business?" check (https://prisqo.vilouraai.com/free-check) and the free SEO audit (https://prisqo.vilouraai.com/free-seo-audit). Paid next step: the $19 Prisqo report at prisqo.vilouraai.com.

Don't quote numbers that aren't on our own pages. No client counts, no results promises.

## The 30-second script

> Hi, is this the owner or manager? Great. This is Jilsha with VilouraAI, a small team right here in Lewisville. I'll be quick.
>
> More customers now ask AI assistants like ChatGPT or Gemini who to call, and AI only names a few businesses. I noticed [hook, said plainly, e.g. "you don't have a website listed on Google"], so I wanted to offer two free things.
>
> First, a free check of whether AI recommends [business] for [niche] in [city]. Second, a free SEO audit of your site and Google listing, emailed to you. No account, no obligation.
>
> If you want the full picture afterwards, our Prisqo report is $19, one time. It shows what AI says across six buyer questions and who it names instead.
>
> Which email should I send the free audit to?

If they say yes: confirm the spelling of the email and the website, thank them, and log it. Then submit the free SEO audit request yourself with their details.

If they're busy: "No problem. What's a better time to call back, morning or afternoon?"

## Objections

**"I'm not interested."**
> Totally fair. The check and the audit are free, and there's nothing to sign up for. Can I just email you the audit so you have it? If not, I'll take you off my list.

**"We get enough business from Google and referrals."**
> That's great to hear. This just shows whether AI assistants name you too, since more people now ask them first. The free check takes a few seconds, and you can decide if it matters.

**"Is this a subscription? What's the catch?"**
> No catch. The check and the audit are free. The Prisqo report is $19 once, with no subscription, and it comes with a 14-day refund if it fails or comes back empty. Only pay if you want it.

## After the call

- Note the outcome next to the lead (interested, call back, not interested, wrong number).
- If they say "don't call again", respect it and mark them so nobody calls twice.
- If they gave an email, send only the free audit they asked for. No other emails without a new yes.
