# Viloura AI offers (for outreach)

Pick the offer that matches each lead's real `need_signals`. Do not invent prices for website, receptionist, or AI agent work. For those, invite a "quick call to scope it."

## Offers

### (a) Prisqo — $19 one-time AI-visibility report
- What: a one-time "Does AI recommend your business?" report.
- URL: https://prisqo.vilouraai.com
- Free check hook: https://prisqo.vilouraai.com/free-check?ref=email
- Price: $19, one time. This is the only offer with a stated price.
- When to pick: the business already has a working website and reviews, but looks weak in AI visibility (not named by AI assistants, thin or generic web presence, no AI-ready content). Use this as the low-friction opener.

### (b) Website build
- What: a new or replacement website for the business.
- Price: do not invent a price. Say a "quick call to scope it."
- When to pick: `need_signals` show no working website, a directory stub only, a suspended or dead site, or a domain that is unrelated or unusable for new customers.

### (c) AI Receptionist
- What: answers every call and books appointments.
- Price: do not invent a price. Say a "quick call to scope it."
- When to pick: `need_signals` show unanswered phones, missed calls, hard-to-reach staff, or reviews about not getting through.

### (d) AI Agent
- What: an AI agent that can handle customer questions and routine tasks beyond just answering the phone (for example web/chat help or repetitive booking questions).
- Price: do not invent a price. Say a "quick call to scope it."
- When to pick: `need_signals` actually support more than a phone answerer — online booking gaps, repetitive customer questions, or a shop that needs an always-on helper on web/chat. Do not default to this.

## Decision rule

1. Read `need_signals` first. Use `recommended_service` and `pitch_angle` as hints, not as a script you copy blindly. The offer must match the real need.
2. If they have a site and reviews but weak AI visibility, lead with (a) the $19 report.
3. If they have no site, a suspended site, or unanswered phones, lead with (b) website and/or (c) AI Receptionist — whichever the signals support. You may name a primary offer and a secondary if both are clearly needed (for example, a suspended site plus missed calls → website + AI receptionist), but write the email or script around one clear ask.
4. Never invent a dollar amount for (b), (c), or (d).
5. Record the chosen offer in the `chosen_offer` column of the segment CSV, and give a one-line reason in the out file.
