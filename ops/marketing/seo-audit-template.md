# Free SEO audit — Example Dental Studio (fictional example)

- **Website:** example-dental-studio.example (fictional)
- **Checked on:** 2026-11-12
- **Checked by:** Prisqo / VilouraAI

## Scores

| Area | Score | One line |
|---|---|---|
| Titles & descriptions | 4/10 | Every title repeats the business name twice; descriptions run long and get cut off. |
| Speed | 6/10 | Homepage is usable but slow on mobile. |
| Schema | 3/10 | No business, price or FAQ markup found. |
| Local & Google Business Profile | 5/10 | Listing exists; hours are missing and no services are listed. |
| AI visibility | 2/10 | AI assistants name a competitor when asked for a dentist nearby. |
| **Overall** | **20/50** | The site is invisible in the places that send new patients: search results, maps, and AI answers. |

## Top 3 findings

### 1. Titles repeat the business name
- **Evidence:** the homepage title, checked 2026-11-12, reads "Example Dental Studio | Example Dental Studio".
- **Why it matters:** the repeated name pushes the words patients search for out of the visible title.

### 2. No schema markup
- **Evidence:** the homepage and services pages, checked 2026-11-12, carry no business, price or FAQ markup.
- **Why it matters:** search engines and AI assistants have to guess the services and prices instead of reading them.

### 3. AI assistants name a competitor
- **Evidence:** in the check run 2026-11-12, the AI answer to "best dentist nearby" named a competing practice, not Example Dental Studio.
- **Why it matters:** patients who ask AI instead of Google never see this business at all.

## What we would fix

1. Rewrite every page title once, keyword first, business name once (homepage, services pages).
2. Add business, price and FAQ schema to the homepage and services pages.
3. Complete the Google Business Profile: hours, services, and a review ask for recent patients.
