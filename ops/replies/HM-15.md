## Live QA before (2026-11-24, https://prisqo.vilouraai.com)

Checked live, read-only, no payment, three pre-filled free-check URLs:

1. `/free-check?category=dentist&city=Irving%2C+TX&name=Meridian+Dental&ref=reply` — result ran automatically. Heading: "AI did not name you in our last check". Body named the 5 Gemini Nov 13, 2026 answers for "best dentist in Irving, TX" and ended "Find out where you stand across 6 buyer questions — and what to fix." CTAs: "Get my report ($19)" under the result and at the bottom, both → `/?ref=freecheck#check` — no category, city or name carried over.
2. `/free-check?category=chiropractor&city=Keller%2C+TX&name=Firm+Foundation+Wellness+Center&ref=reply` — **Keller IS covered live**: "Keller, TX" is in the city dropdown (pre-selected) and a saved Nov 13 snapshot exists (Keller Family Chiropractic, Performance Chiropractic Clinic, Thrive Chiropractic, Alpha & Omega Chiropractic). No "not checked yet" state. This contradicts `ops/marketing/replies-variant-a-1.md`, which says "Our free check doesn't cover Keller yet" — see Leftovers.
3. `/free-check?category=dentist&city=Garland%2C+TX&name=Test+Dental` — honest "Not checked yet" state, listing the 10 dentist cities with saved answers. No fake list. CTA → `/?ref=freecheck#check` only.
4. Homepage checkout at `/?category=dentist&city=Irving%2C+TX&name=Meridian+Dental&ref=freecheck#check` — page landed scrolled to #check, but **all checkout fields were blank** (website, category, city, business name). The checkout form did not read URL params, and the free-check CTAs did not pass any.

Also observed: `ref=` is stripped from the address bar after a free-check result loads (`free-check.js` `run()` rewrites the URL without it). See Leftovers.

## What changed

### `free-check.js` (result screen only; form fields, names, testids untouched)
- The primary CTA after every result now carries the result through to checkout: `/?category=<label>&city=<city>&name=<name>&ref=<incoming ref or freecheck>#check`. The incoming `ref` (e.g. `reply`) is captured at load, before `run()` rewrites the address bar, and preserved on the checkout link.
- After a successful result (named or not named), a short explainer now says what the $19 report adds — facts from live `pricing.html` only: 6 buyer-style questions, visibility score (0–100), who AI names instead, which sources it cites, every prompt and answer in full; $19 one-time, no account, no subscription; full refund within 14 days if the report fails or comes back empty. Secondary links: "See pricing" (`/pricing`) and "See a sample report" (`/sample/results`, already in the sitemap).
- "Not checked yet" (uncovered city) still shows the honest fallback and the list of covered cities for that category, and now also links to `/pricing` and `/free-seo-audit` next to the primary CTA. No city was added to the free check; no snapshot was fabricated.
- "Check unavailable right now" (data fetch failed) keeps its copy and now also links to `/pricing`.
- No new URLs. No LP-2 homepage restyle. Share links unchanged.

### `prisqo-check.js` (homepage checkout only)
- New prefill: on load, `?category=`, `?city=` and `?name=` fill the checkout fields `#category`, `#city` and `#brand` — but only when the field is empty, so a visitor's own typing is never overwritten. Form ids, names, `action="#"`, `method="post"`, notice and links unchanged; no engine or Stripe change.

- HM-15 claimed: row + details moved Next → Doing, `owner: muse`, `status: doing`.
