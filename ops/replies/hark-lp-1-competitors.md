## Notes

- Peec AI's homepage price: as of Oct 2026 the pricing page lists plan limits with a monthly/annual
  toggle but no public prices in the copy read; third-party reads report Starter $95/mo. The research
  file records both so the landing page never has to guess.
- Am I On AI and Scrunch homepages could not be read directly on 12 Nov 2026 (bot wall; unrelated
  content served). Their rows rely on third-party reads of their own pages dated Oct–Nov 2026 and say so.
- What the study says to steal: hero hierarchy with one outcome, proof near the CTA, a free check
  above the fold, an on-page FAQ, a sticky mobile CTA, and a plain-English contrast table — not any
  competitor's copy, logos, or screenshots.
