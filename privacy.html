Privacy policy

What we collect. The website, category, city and business name you enter, and the report we generate from them. At checkout, Stripe asks for your email and your payment details; your card details go to Stripe, and we never see them.

How we use it. To run your check and show your report. Your report is stored so its web address keeps working. We don't send marketing email.

Who else sees it. Stripe processes the payment. Groq runs the AI model with live web search that powers the check. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It uses no cookies and collects no personal data. We run no ad trackers, and we do not sell your details or your report.

How long it is kept, and deletion. Reports are kept so their addresses keep working. To ask for your report to be deleted, email vilouraai1@gmail.com and include your report address.
