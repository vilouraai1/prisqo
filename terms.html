Terms of service

What you buy. One visibility report for one website, category and city. You pay once, at secure checkout. There is no subscription and no account.

What the check does. We ask 6 buyer-style questions to one AI model with live web search, and show whether you were recommended, who was recommended instead, and which sources were cited. Your report. Your report is stored so its web address keeps working. Bookmark that address — it is how you come back to your report. We do not email reports. We don't send marketing email.

Payment. Your card is processed by Stripe; we never see your card details. One payment covers one report. If the check fails before producing a report, reloading the page it sent you back to retries the check once with the same payment. If your report fails or comes back empty, email us within 14 days and we'll refund you in full. Email vilouraai1@gmail.com and include your check id or report address.

Your data. We do not sell your report or the details you entered. See the privacy policy for what is stored and how to ask for deletion.
